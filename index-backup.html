Diesel / petrol ECU concepts
DIESEL

Start of injection (SOI)

Crank-angle timing, RPM and load axes where applicable, start-of-injection advance, temperature corrections and operating limits.

DIESEL

Injection duration

Duration / energising-time models, injected quantity conversion, voltage compensation and injector characterisation.

COMMON RAIL

Injection events

Pilot, main and post-injection scheduling, rail-pressure dependency, dwell and event interaction where supported.

PETROL

Fuel and spark

Injection timing, pulse width, lambda targets, ignition advance, knock control and load-dependent corrections.

EDC15P+ unit-injector systems do not use the same rail-pressure and injection-event controls as common-rail ECUs. Petrol ignition maps apply only to suitable spark-ignition ECUs.
